የጥበብ: የምርምርና የፍልስፍና መሠረት ፊደል ነው

እያንዳንዱ ፊደል ስድስት ስድስት አናጋሪ ፊደል ይከተለዋል፡፡ ራሱን የመጀመሪያውን ፊደል ጨምሮ ሰባት ይሆናል፡፡ ሰባት ሊሆን የቻለውም ድሮ የነቢያት አቈጣጠር በሰባት በሰባት ይቈጠር ስለ ነበር ነው፡፡ ማለት አሁን በዐሥር በዐሥር እንደ ምንቆጥር ሁሉ ወይም ሙሉ ቊጥር እንደ ሚሆን ሁሉ በዚያን ጊዜም ፯ ሙሉ ቊጥር ነበር ማለት ነው፡፡ ፊደል፡ አኀዝ ቊጥር፡ ግዕዝና አማርኛ በአክሱም ዘመነ መንግሥት ተመሠረተ

የግዕዝ ፊደልና ቊጥር፡ ፍድለታት (ፊደላት)

ትርጒም፡-……

አንኪ ታቦት ከወዴት መጣሽ፡ ወዴትስ ትኄጂያለሽ፡ መድኀኒት ሁኚ፡፡ ከፍ ከፍ ያለ የእግዚአብሔር ንጉሥ የሕጉ ታቦት ነሽ፡፡ ከዚያ በኋላ ምንሊክ ወደ ሃገሩ ደረሰ፡፡

እናቱ የአዜብ ንግሥት ሳባም በብዙ ደስታ ተቀበለችው፡፡ እስከ 40 ቀንም በዓል አደረጉለት፡፡ ቊጥር የሌላቸዉ ከብቶችና በጎች ታረዱ፡፡ በሳባዕ ምድርም ቤተ መቅደስ ሠርታ ታቦቷን አስቀመጠቻት፡፡ በአደባባይዋም ለካህናት ሰፊ ቦታ ሰጠቻቸዉ፡፡ 12 ቋንቋዎች ባሉባት በከተማዋ በሁሉ ግዛትዋ ቊጥራቸዉ 12 የሚሆን ነገደ እስራኤል ባሉበት ሁሉ ምንሊክን አነገሠችዉ፡፡ ቋንቋዎቹም እነዚህ ናቸዉ፡-

ስለ ኪነ ጥበባት አመጣትና ታሪክ

ስለ የአማርኛ ፊደል የአማርኛ ቊጥር የአማርኛ ቋንቋ የሚሉት ጸሐፊያንና አንባቢያን ግማሹ ታሪኩን ስለማያውቅ ሲሆን ተንኮለኞች ሆን ብለው ባወሩለት ተመሥርቶ ነው፡፡ ግማሹና አብዛኛው ፖለቲከኛ ግን ሆን ብሎ ታሪክን ለማዛባትና ለማበላሸት በሰው አእምሮ ውስጥ ስለ ሚሽሎከሎክ ነው፡፡

ትግራዊያን እንደሆኑ ከአክሱም ውድቀት በኋላ በአጋጠማቸዉ ከባድ የታሪክ ክሥተት ከኅብረተ ሰቡ የተወሰኑ ቢማሩም መተኮስና መታኮስ እንጂ ታሪክን ሊያዛቡ ይቅርና እውነተኛውን ታሪክ ለመግለጽና ለማብራራት ልምድ የላቸዉም፡፡ መማር መጻፍ፡ ማንበብና መመራመር የኅብረተ ሰብ ዕድገት መደኀኒት መሆኑ ቢታወቅም የመጸፍም ይሁን የመቀስቀስ ልምድ የላቸዉም፡፡ ህዝቡ ከሰብአዊነት ወደ ምንነት እንዴት እንደ ተቀየረ የሚያድነው አልተገኘም፡፡

ወያኔ እንደሆነ በመንግሥት ላይ ሳለ ለራሱ ሥልጣንና ክብር ደከመ እንጂ ህዝቡን በሴፍቲ ኔት ዓሥሮት ነው ሃገርን ገድሎ ለራሱ የሞተው፡፡ የትግራይ ህዝብ ያውቃል አማኝ ነው ይባላል እንጂ ምንም አያውቅም፡፡ በስመ እምነት በአጠቃላይ ራሱን የማያድን ተገዶም ወዶም ተኴሽ አረበኛ ብቻ ከመሆኑም በላይ ያልተማረ መነኩሴ የሚያታልለው ህዝብ ነው፡፡ በፍጹም ሞኝ ነው፡፡ አንድ መሓይም ተነሥቶ አምላክን አገኘሁት አንዲት ሴት ተነሥታ ፈጣሪን አማከርኩት ሲለው አምኖት ለልጆቹና ለራሱ የሚበላውን የሌለው ጦሙን እያደረ ሥራ ፈት ምእመናንን የሚመግብ በሰዎች ሥልጣን መንግሥተ ሰማይ እገባለሁ ብሎ የሚያምን ሞኝ ነው፡፡ የሚዳን በእምነትና በሥራ ብቻ ነው፡፡ የፈለገውን ልብስ ቢቀይር ቢያሳምር ስምን ቢያወጣ በሰው መዳን የለም፡

የፈረንጅ ፊደልና ቊጥር የእንግሊዘኛ ፊደል ተብሎ በዓለም ህዝብ እንዲታወቅ የሆነው በቅኚ ግዛትም በወታደራዊ ኃይልም ቀደም ብሎው እንግሊዛዊያን ዓለምን ሲያስጨንቁአት ስለ ነበር የእንግሊዝ ተባሉ እንጂ ቊጥሩ የዓረብ ነው፡፡ ቊጥሩ ከዚያ በፊት ትንሽ ቢሞከርም ከ770ዎች ጀምሮ መሐመድ ጃፋር ክሕዋርዝሚ የተባለ ኢራቃዊ የመሠረተው ነው፡፡ በተለይ 0ን (ዚዶን) ፈጥረሮ ቊጥርን ቊጥር ያደረገው እሱ ነው፡፡ የአማርኛ ፊደልም፡ የአማርኛ ቊጥርም የአማራኛ ቋንቋም በተመሳሳይ ባለፉ ዘመናት ገዢዎችና ባለ ሥልጣናት በአብዛኛውና ከፍተኛውን ሥልጣን ለረጂም ዘመን በአማራዎች ቁጠጥር ሥር ሰለ ነበር እንደ እንግሊዙ ቋንቋና ቊጥር የኛም በውስጣዊ ተፅዕኖ የአማራ ነው እንዲባል ድጋፍ ሰጪ ነበር፡፡

ለትግሬና ለሌላ ሰብአ ብሔር ትንሽ ሥልጣን ለታዋቂ የሰብአ ብሔሩ ሰው ተቆንጠራ እየተሰጠች እንደ ባለ መብት በማስመሰል እየተገረፈም እየታሠረም ይኖር ነበር፡፡ አሁን ሁሉም አልፎአል፡፡ የምሥራቁም የምዕራቡም የሰሜኑም የደቡቡም ኢትዮጵያ ቋንቋና ባህል ቊጥር ሁሉ መገናኛ እስከ ሆነ ድረስ መርጠንም ወይም ሁሉንም በኅበረት መገልገል ይገባል፡፡ ኅብረት ኃይል ነው ኅብረት ኤኮኖሚ ነው፡፡ ኅብረት ትልቅነት ነው፡፡ መለያየት ድኅነት ነው፡፡ ደካማነት ነው፡፡ በማንኛውም ቦታ ያለው የሃገር ንብረትና ታሪክ ባህል የኔ ነው ብሎ መጠበቅ መንከባከብ ይገባል፡፡ የኅብረተ ሰብ መበላለጥ የነበረ ቢሆንም በዓፄ ኃይለ ሥላሴ ጊዜ አንድነትን ትንሽ አመልክቶ ነበር፡፡ ጎፈሬው ደርግ ተነሥቶ አደፈረሰው፡ በመቀጠል ኢሕአዴግ ገብቶ ሁሉንም ነገር አፈረሰው፡፡

ብልጽግናም ልብ ቢል መልካም ነው፡፡ ህዝብ ከሌለ ሜዳና ተራራ የፈጣሪ ውበት ናቸዉ፡፡ ህዝብ ባሕር ነው መንግሥት ዓሣ ነው፡፡ ባሕር ከሌለ ዓሣው ሕይወት አይኖረውም፡፡ ባሕሩ እንዳይቀንስ እንዳይደርቅ ዛፍ ሣር ያስፈልጋል፡፡ ህዝብ እንዲኖር በአካፋ የሚዛቅ ባይሆንም መጥኖ በጭልፋ የሚጨለፍ ሰላም ዲሞክራሲ ቢኖር አስፈላጂና ለሁሉም መልካም ነው፡፡ ለመኝታ ቤት ለእግር መርገጫ መሬት ተፈጥሮአዊ ስጦታ ነው፡፡ ይህ የሚሰጥና የሚነፈግ አልነበረም፡፡ ሰዎች ግን ያለ ተፈጥሮአቸዉ ለማድረግ በጣም ይደፍራሉ፡፡

On the Origin and History of the Arts

Of the writers and readers who speak of “the Amharic alphabet”, “the Amharic numerals” and “the Amharic language”, half do so because they do not know the history, and rest on what the crafty have deliberately put about. But the other half — indeed the greater part, the politicians — do so because they deliberately worm their way into people’s minds in order to distort and spoil history.

As for the Tigrayans, because of the grave events of history that befell them after the fall of Aksum, though some of their society have been educated, they know only shooting and firing at one another; let alone distorting history, they have no practice in setting out and explaining the true history. Though it is known that learning, writing, reading and research are the remedy for a society’s growth, they have no practice either in writing or in awakening others. No one has been found to rescue the people from the way they were turned from humanity into nothingness.

As for the Woyane, while it was in government it laboured only for its own power and glory; binding the people with the Safety Net, it killed the country and died for its own sake. The people of Tigray are said to be knowing and faithful, but they know nothing. In the name of faith they are a people who, on the whole, do not save themselves — beyond being, forced or willing, nothing but marksmen and fighters — a people whom an unlearned monk deceives. They are utterly foolish. When an ignorant man rises up and says, “I have found God,” or a woman rises up and says, “I have taken counsel with the Creator,” they believe it; having nothing to eat for themselves and their children, they go to bed hungry while feeding idle believers — fools who believe they will enter the Kingdom of Heaven by the authority of men. One is saved only by faith and by works. Whatever robe a man changes into or adorns himself with, whatever name he takes, there is no salvation through man.

The European alphabet and numerals came to be known by the people of the world as “English” because, earlier, the English had oppressed the world through colonial rule and military power; that is why they were called English — but the numerals are Arab. Although there had been some small attempts at numerals before that, it was an Iraqi named Mohammed Jafar Khwarizmi who founded them, from the 770s onward. It was he above all who, by creating 0 (zero), made number truly number. In the same way, because in past ages the rulers and officials — for the most part, and in the highest offices for a long time — were under the control of the Amhara, this lent support, through internal influence, to the Amharic alphabet, the Amharic numerals and the Amharic language being called Amhara, just as the language and numerals were called English.

To the Tigrayans and to other nationalities a little power would be pinched off and handed to some well-known person of that nationality, making a show of their having rights, while the people lived on, flogged and imprisoned. Now all of that has passed. So long as all the languages, cultures and numerals of Ethiopia — east, west, north and south — are means of communication, we ought to make use of them, whether by choosing among them or all of them together in unity. Unity is strength; unity is economy. Unity is greatness. Division is poverty; it is weakness. The nation’s property, history and culture, wherever they are, should be guarded and cared for by each of us, saying, “It is mine.” Though there was inequality in society, in the time of Emperor Haile Selassie a little unity had been shown. The shaggy-haired Derg rose up and muddied it; then the EPRDF came in and tore everything down.

It would be good if the Prosperity Party, too, took heed. Without the people, plain and mountain are only the Creator’s beauty. The people are the sea; the government is the fish. Without the sea, the fish will have no life. So that the sea neither shrinks nor dries up, trees and grass are needed. For the people to live, peace and democracy — if not heaped out by the shovelful, then measured out by the ladleful — are needful and good for all. Land on which to sleep and to set one’s foot is a gift of nature; it was never a thing to be given or withheld. But people are very bold in doing what goes against their nature.

From Adam to Noah, the children of Adam were brothers. From Noah to where we stand, we too — people come from wherever they may — are brothers. Wherever we come from, we are the children of Shem, of Japheth and of Ham; apart from these, no other human being has been created in the world. And so, though the line of the generations may seem far apart, blood is blood and flesh is flesh: in bone and flesh we are brothers. Therefore no one should be cruel to his brother, to his sister, to a fellow human being. Wherever a person comes from, he ought to be honoured for his very nature, as a human being.
