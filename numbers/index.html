ክፍል ፪Part 2

ቁጥሮችNumbers

የኢትዮጵያ ቅዱሳት የቁጥር ሥርዓቶች — ትርጉማቸውና መንፈሳዊ ፋይዳቸው።The sacred numerical systems of Ethiopia — their meaning and spiritual significance.

ከአሁን ቀደም አኀዝ ቊጥርን ማሻሻልና ማሳደግ የሞከሩ ኢትዮጵያዊያንEthiopians Who Have Tried to Improve and Extend the Ge’ez Numerals

ሙከራቸዉ የሚደነቅ ሲሆን፡ በነበረበት ዘመን ታሪካዊና ሃገራዊ ቊጥር እንደ መሆኑ መጠን፡ አሁን ቊጥሩ ከ፩፻፼ (ከ1000000) ቤት በላይ ከፍ እያለ ሲኄድ የሁሉም ባለ ሙያዎች ውሳኔ ሊስማማ አልቻለም፡፡ የኸውም ስለ ሃገር ስለ ህዝብ በኅብረት መሥራትና መወሰን ይገባ ነበር፡፡ ስለ አገር ጉዳይ በጥናት ተደግፎ እየቀነሱ እየጨመሩ በኅብረት መሥራት እንደ ሚገባ ይታመናል፡፡ የሁሉም መነሻቸዉ የአለቃ ኪዳነ ወልድ ክፍሌ እንደ ሆነም በሥራቸዉ ይረጋገጣል፡፡

Their efforts are admirable; in its own time this was a historic and national system of numbers. Yet as the numbers rise beyond the place of ፩፻፼ (1,000,000), the conclusions of the specialists have failed to agree. On a matter that concerns the nation and its people, they ought to have worked and decided together; it is right that national questions be worked on jointly, supported by research, taking away and adding as needed. Their own work confirms that all of them took Aleqa Kidane Wold Kifle as their starting point.

ይሁን እንጂ አለቃ ኪዳነ ወልድ እንደ ሌላ ጥናታቸዉ በአኀዝ ቍጥር ላይ አላተኮሩበትም፡፡ ከእሳቸዉ በፊት ሦስቱን የቍጥር ሕግ ያሟላ ከመቶ እልፍ ቤት በላይ የያዘ ዋቢ መጻሕፍትን ስለ አላገኙ እንደ መሰላቸዉ ነው ነክተውት ያለፉት፡፡ ለምሳሌ መዝገበ ፊደል 1926 ዓ. ም. በሚል መጽሐፋቸዉና መጽሐፈ ሰዋስው ወግሥ ወመዝገበ ቃላት ሐዲስ 1948 ዓ. ም. በሚለው ተመሳሳይ ውሳኔ አያመለክትም፡፡ በ፼፼ (አእላፍ) ትእልፊትና ምእልፊት ላይ ውሳኔያቸዉ የተለያየ ሆኖ ይገኛል፡፡ ስለ ዚህ ስለ አኀዝ ቍጥር አላሰቡበትም ነበር ለማለት ይቻላል፡፡ ምክንያቱም ሌላ ጥናታቸዉ የተከበረ የሚደነቅ ስለ ሆነ ነው፡፡ ስለ ሌላዎች ከእሳቸዉ መጽሐፍ እየገለበጡ የጻፉት ስለ ሆኑ ብዙም የሚያነጋግር አይደለም፡፡ በባሕረ ቀመር ከ፩፻፼ ቤት በላይ የቀረበው ጥናት፡ የራሴ ግኝት እንደ ሆነ መታወቅ አለበት፡፡

Aleqa Kidane Wold, however, did not give the numerals the attention he gave his other studies. Having found no earlier reference work that satisfied the three laws of number and went beyond the place of a hundred elf, he touched on the subject as it seemed to him and moved on. His Mezgebe Fidel (1926 E.C.) and his Metshafe Sewasew Wegis Wemezgebe Qalat Haddis (1948 E.C.), for example, do not reach the same conclusion: they differ on ፼፼ (a’elaf), ትእልፊት and ምእልፊት. One may say, then, that he had not thought the numerals through — for the rest of his scholarship is respected and admired. As for the others, since they wrote by copying from his book, there is little to discuss. It should be known that the study presented in Bahre Kemer beyond the place of ፩፻፼ is my own discovery.

Let it first be established that I hold a certificate of ownership issued by the Ethiopian Intellectual Property Office, No. 1/1/1/2/0041, registered on 03/09/2007 E.C. Be advised that, from that date, anyone who writes the Bahre Kemer numbers beyond the place of ፩፻፼ (one hundred elf) in a distorted form, outside the rules of the Bahre Kemer number system, is liable under the law.

The organisation of modern young people called Mahibere Kidusan did not grow up in the church’s courtyard and precincts; yet, given its foundation and its content, the day will not be far off when it says, “It is I who represent the Church.” Like a defenceless blade of grass that broke through the order of monasticism and entered the sanctuary armed with its ABCD pistol, it seemed a guardian of heritage and a defender of culture — and one might indeed think so. Its efforts, such as lifting the morale of the traditional teachers and, if only a little, warding off immediate dangers, show that even now it is outdoing the monastic administration in consciousness — and corrupting it. On the other hand, its destructiveness is being confirmed in public: it creates obstacles that keep the generation from advancing the growth of its numbers through a single national scholarship and wisdom. The book (Bahre Hassab) is otherwise a work of national scholarship; whom does it benefit to skim off a table of numerals, paste it on the last page and so mislead a generation? It can only harm everyone. Pasting numerals onto the last page out of habit will no longer pass: with the scholarly book Bahre Kemer, that is over and done. Think about it even now: wading unknowingly into such a hazard benefits neither the nation nor anyone. Had it been something supported by research and worth debating, a discussion would do no harm. But this is no more than a weed sown by birds; for the sake of the generation, it would be a worthy national gesture to issue a statement on the table of numerals pasted at the end of your book. And if anyone holds that it is correct, bring us together on a platform built in the spirit of Ethiopian identity, so that we may reach an understanding and choose what is true for the nation.

ይህ ምእልፊታት ወደ ዘመናዊ የማባዛት ስሌት ሲለወጥ፡ 10000 × 10000 × 10000 × 10000 × 10000 × 10000 × 10000 ይሆናል፡፡ ተደጋገሚ ጥናት ቢከሃኄድበት በሁሉም መንገድ ሊያሠራ የሚችል የተሟላ ዕድገት ይኖረዋል ብሎአል የሊቃውንት ጉባዔ፡፡

Converted to modern multiplication, this ምእልፊታት becomes 10000 × 10000 × 10000 × 10000 × 10000 × 10000 × 10000. The Council stated that, with repeated study, it would have a complete progression that works in every way.

የአኀዝ ቁጥሩን ሥርዓት በማሳጠር ትክክለኛ ሕጉን ጠብቆአል፡፡ በዐሥር በዐሥር የሚያድገው ሥርዓትንም የጠበቀ ነው፡፡ ፍቹ ግን የአለቃ ኪዳነ ወልድ ሥርዓትን የተከተለ እንጂ መሠረታዊዉን አልተከተለም፡፡ ምክንያቱም ሦስቱን የቁጥር ሕጎችን አላሙዋላምና ነው፡፡

By abbreviating the numeral system it keeps the correct rule, and it preserves growth by tens. Its reading, however, follows the system of Aleqa Kidane Wold rather than the fundamental one — for it does not fulfil the three laws of number.

አንድ እልፋት ወይም ቴን ክዋድሪሊየን (Ten quadrillion)፡ ከትሪሊየን ቀጥሎ ማለት ነው፡፡ ፩፼፼፼፼፡ ይህ ማለት አንድ እልፋት ነው፡፡ በተጨማሪ ባሕረ ቀመር ገጽ 8 በሕረ ቀመር ሁለተኛ ገጽ 61 ተመልከት፡፡

One elfat — ten quadrillion — the order that follows the trillions. ፩፼፼፼፼ means one elfat. See also Bahre Kemer, p. 8, and Bahre Kemer, Volume Two, p. 61.

የቁጥሩ መሠረት ከሆኑ ዐሠራ ሦስት ንዑሳን መራኁት ከሚባሉ የመጀመርያዎች 4ቱ እነዚህ ናቸዉ፡፡ በተጨማሪ ባሕረ ቀመር፡ 2003 ዓ. ም. ገጽ 8 እና 180፡ ባሕረ ቀመር ሁለተኛ 2004 ዓ. ም. ገጽ 61 ተመልከት፡፡ ከ16280000 ድርድር (ዲጂትስ) ውስጥ ዘርፍ ካልሆነ በቀር የቁጥርም ይሁን የፊች አንዲት ቃል የተደገመች አትገኝም፡፡ የቁጥሩ ሕግም በሁለት ዘዴ ማመሳከር እንደ ሚቻል ተገልጾአል፡፡

These are the first four of the thirteen ne’usan merakhut (“minor keys”) on which the number system is founded. See also Bahre Kemer (2003 E.C.), pp. 8 and 180, and Bahre Kemer, Volume Two (2004 E.C.), p. 61. Across 16,280,000 digits, apart from the branch markers, not a single word — of numeral or of reading — is repeated. It has also been shown that the rules of the system can be verified by two methods.

ከላይ ከዓራቱ አምዶች ሁለተኛው በግዕዝ የተነበበው ሁሉንም የኢትዮጵያ ቋንቋዎች ይወክላል፡፡ ይህ በዐሥር በዐሥር ያደገና በቅኔ ቤት ርባታ ሕግ የተመሠረተ ሦስቱን የቁጥር ሕጎችን አሙዋልቶ የተመሠረተ ነው፡፡

Of the four columns above, the second — read in Ge’ez — represents all the languages of Ethiopia. This system grows by tens, rests on the rules of conjugation (rubata) of the Qene school, and fulfils the three laws of number.

የራሳችንን ቊጥር ሳናስተካክል የፈረንጁን ቊጥር ማስተካከል እንችላለን፡፡ ከዚህም በላይ እንደ ምናየው ከአንድ ቦታ በስተቀር የዓረቢክ ቊጥሩ ልክ ነው፡፡ የፈረንጁን ሥርዓት ይዞ ተደርድሮአል፡፡ ወደ እኛ የአኀዝ ቊጥርና ወደ ግዕዙ ስንገባ ግን ዕንግዶች መሆናችንን ሥራችን ያረጋግጥልናል ያጋልጠናል፡፡ ይህ ብቻም አይደለም የአብዛኛዎቹ በዚሁ መልክ የተሠራ ነው፡፡ በስሕተቱም ቢስማሙኮ መልካም ነበር፡፡ ያለው ችግር በስሕተቱም አይስማሙም፡፡ በጣም የሚያሳዝነው ግን ተተኪ ትውልድ በአንድ ሃገራዊ የቊትር አቅጣጫ ተመሥርቶ ምርምሩን እንዳያሳድግ በየጓዳው ድሪቶና አምቡላ የሚመስል ኮተት እየቆለሉ አቅጣጫን ማሳጣታቸዉን ነው፡፡ በጠላት ይሁን ባለ ማወቅ የነበረው ነባር የቁጥር ዕድገት ጠፍቶአል፡፡ አሁን በዚሁ መነሻነት ጥናታችንነን መቀጠለል ይገባል እንጂ ከፍተኛ የቁጥር ይዘት በኢትዮጵያ አልነበረም ብሎ መደምደም ግን በጣም የሚከብድ ነው፡፡

We are able to set the foreigners’ numbers in order without having set our own in order. As we see above, the Arabic numerals are correct except in one place; they are arranged following the foreign system. But when we come to our own numerals and to the Ge’ez, our work confirms — and exposes — that we are strangers to them. Nor is this an isolated case: most of the works were made the same way. It would have been something had they at least agreed in their errors; the trouble is that they do not agree even in those. Saddest of all, by heaping up clutter like rags and dregs in every back room, they leave the next generation without direction — unable to ground itself in a single national approach to number and so advance its research. Whether through enemies or through ignorance, the old, established progression of numbers has been lost. We must now continue our study from this starting point; to conclude that Ethiopia never possessed a higher system of numbers would be a grave thing indeed.

As the Ge’ez numerals are a national heritage, I call on any body — governmental or private — that considers this its concern to gather all Ethiopians who have written on the numerals, or who hold the matter to be their concern, and to convene a conference to extend, study or decide upon them.
