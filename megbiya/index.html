መግቢያና ቀጣይ ማሳሠቢያIntroduction, and a Note on What Follows

ንቃተ ሕሊና ቀስ በቀስ ከማሠብ ይፈጠራል፡ ይመነጫል፡ ይመሠረታል።Consciousness is born of thinking, little by little: it springs from thought, and is founded upon it.

ቅኔና ሒሣብ

ይህ ገጽ፡ ገጸ ቅኔና ገጸ ባሕረ ቀመር ሆኖ ይቀጥላል። ንቃተ ሕሊና ቀስ በቀስ ከማሠብ ይፈጠራል፡ ይመነጫል፡ ይመሠረታል። ቅኔም የሒሣብ ምንጭ ነው። ስለ ሆነም ንቃተ ሕሊናና ፍልስፍና ከቅኔ ጋር የተዛመዱ ናቸዉ። ግን የቅኔው ንቃተ ሕሊና ከሥራ ላይ እንዳይውል ለራሱና ለሃገር የሚበጀውን አቅጣጫ አመልካች አላገኘም፡፡

መንፈሳዊ ትምህርት

ዕቅዱ በዚህ ዙርያ ለማጠንጠን ነው። መንፈሳዊና ሥጋዊ ንቃተ ሐሊና ወሳኝ ነው። ክሁሉ በፊት ፈጣሪዉን ከፍጡራን ለይቶ ማወቅ ግድ ይለዋል። ኢትዮጵያዊያን ምእመናንን ይህን ለይቶ የሚያስተምራቸዉ አላገኙም፡፡ ለቅዱስ ወንጌል አስተማሪነት የሚመለመሉ ሕፃናትም አዲስ አበባ መንፈሳዊ ኮለጅ ደርሰዉ ሲመለሱ ፈጣሪያቸዉን ለይተዉ የሚያውቁበት ሳይሆን የባሰ የሚዝረከረኩበት ተጭነውና ልምዳዊውን ባህል አጠናክረዉ የሚመሱበት ነው፡፡ ማን እንደ ፈጠራቸዉ እንክዋን የራሳቸዉን ፈጣሪ ለይተዉ የማያውቁና አምልኮ ጣዖትን ለሞምላት የምእመናንን አእምሮ የሚያሟሹ ናቸዉ፡፡ ጥረታቸዉም አንበሳና ነብር እንስሳትን ሰብረዉ ለመብላት የሚጥሩትን ያህል ነው፡፡ ፈጣሪንንና ፍጡርን በእኩልነት አዳምረዉ እንዲያመልኩ በሰከነ ቅዱስ መንፈስ ሳይሆን ርኩስ መንፈስ በሚመስል በመንጫጫት ለማስረዳት የሚሞክሩ ናቸዉ፡፡

ትምህርት ወደ ሰው አይተላለፍም

አሁን የሚቀርበው ትምህርት ወደ ሰው አይተላለፍም፡፡ የቆሰለውን ልብ አይፈውስም፡፡ አስተማሪዎቹ የመንፈስ ቅዱስ እንስራ ለመሆን አልደከሙምና ነው፡፡ የመንፈስ ቅዱስ እንስራ መሆን ምን እንደ ሚሠራ የመንፈስ ቅዱስ እንስራ የሆነውን ሰው ብቻ ያውቀዋል፡፡ በተለያየ እምንት፡ እምነት ነኝ በሚሉ ሁሉ በየኮርነሩ መንፈሳዊ ትምህርት ይሰጣል፡፡ በተለይ በተዋሕዶ እምነት የሚቀርበው ያለ ትምህርት ትዕግሥትን የተላበሰ በርቀት የተሞገሰ የቤተ ክርስቲያንዋ የአባቶች ባህል ሳይሆን ከጎረቤት ተበድረዉ ሰውነታቸዉ በመንፈስ የተሰበረና የረቀቀ ስለ አይደለና ስለ ጎረና እንደ አንበሳና እንደ በሬ በሚጮሁበት ጊዜ በየሰፈሩ ህዝቡ እስከ ሚማረር ይጮሃሉ፡፡ መጮህ ዕውቀት፡ ማስረዳትና ማሳመን ሊሆን አይችልም፡፡

ወጣቱና ኅብረተ ሰቡ

በየታክሲው፡ በየገበያውና በህዝባዊ ቦታዎች ስንኄድና ኅብረተ ሰቡን፡ በተለይ ወጣቶችን ስናይ እናቶችንና አባቶችን፡ ርጉዞችንና ደካሞችን ሲገፈትሩአቸዉና ሲሰድቡአቸዉ ያየና የሰማ ሰው እንኳንስ ኢትዮጵያ ውስጥ የተወለዱና ያደጉ ሊመስሉ የቱሪስትን ያህል ኢትዮጵያን የጎበኙ አይመስሉም፡፡ ውሰጡን ገብታችሁ የማታውቁት ሰዎች ነቀፋና ማቃለል ሊመስላችሁ ይችላል፡፡ ግን አይደለም እውነቱን ለማያውቅ ማሳወቅና ለነሱም መንፈሳዊ ተግሳፅና ወገናዊ ምክር ይሆናል ተብሎ ስለታመነበት ነው፡፡

ለሃገር ዕድገት

ለሃገር ዕድገትን ለወጣቶች ንቃትን ሊሰጥ የሚችል በማንኛውም የፍልስፍና ንቃተ ሕሊና ፅንሰ ሐሣብ የተመሠረተ ሥነ ጽሑፍንም ሆነ ድምፃዊ ምክርን ያስተናግዳል፡ ያንፀባርቃል። ሀ ወይም አንድ ብሎ የንቃተ ሕሊና ፍልስፍና ፅንሰ ሐሣብን ለመለማመድ ነው። ፍልስፍናንና ንቃተ ሕሊናን ለማስተማር መማርን ይጠይቃል። ለመማርና ለማወቅ ግን የሚከለክል ሥልጣንና ሥርዓት ኖሮ አያውቅም። በከፍተኛ ተቅዋማት ውስጥ ንቃተ ሕሊናና ፍልስፍና አልነበረም፡ አሁንም የለም ለማለት ሳይሆን ይህንኑን ለመለማመድ ነው።

ኪነ ጥበባት

ቢሆነም የሚጎላው በኪነ ጥበባት አመካኝነት ነው። በዓለማችን ውስጥ ያለው ዕንቅስቃሴ ሁሉ ጥበብ ነውና። ቀድመዉ ትንሽ የነቁና በአስተዳደሩ አከባቢ የሚኮለኮሉ፡ ኪነ ጥበብንና ፍልስፍናን በአዲስነቱ ጨረር ሲያመለክት አመፅ ብለዉ ይጠሩታል፡፡ ለራሱና ለሚቀጥለው ኅብረተ ሰብ የብርሃን ምልክት ጨረር ቢሆንም ለማያውቁት ግን አመፅ ነውና፡፡ ኪነ ጥበባት (Arts) የተባሉት ታላላቅ ምሥጢራዊ ተፈጥሮን ማንፀባረቅ ከሚችሉ ጥቂቶችን ብንመለከት፡ ቀደም ብለዉ የተገኙ ፍልስፍናዎች፡ ሥዕል፡ ሙዚቃ፡ ድራማ፡ ቅኔ፡ ሥነ ጽሑፍ እና የመሳሰሉት ናቸዉ።

የኪነ ጥበብ ሰዎች ሙያ

የኪነ ጥበብ ሰዎች ሙያ ከንቃተ ሕሊና አልፎ በማንኛውም የዕድገትና የሥልጣኔ ጉዞ ጥንካሬ እንደ ብረትና ስሚንቶ ሆኖ የሚያገግል ነው። በተለይም ካለው ኅብረተ ሰብ በሕሊና ተለይቶ ወይም አመፆ በመንፈሳዊም ይሁን በሥጋዊ ወይም በሳይንስ መንቀሳቀስ ማለት ነው። የኪነ ጥበብ እንቅስቃሴ ሁል ጊዜም ሥውር ነው ጉልህ ሆኖ አይታይም። ወጣቱን ይህን የመሰለ አቅጣጫ ካላመለከቱት ካለበት ቁጭ ብሎ አገርን ሊያሳድግ ይቅርና አሱ ራሱ የሃግር ሽከም ይሆናል። ሃገር ማለት፡ ከኅብረተ ሰቡ ነጥረዉ በሚወጡ ዜጎቿ ትጠበቃለች ታድጋለች እጀጂ በአንድ ፕሬዚዳንት ወይም ጠቅላይ ሚኒስቲር ተንጠልጥላ የምትኖር ወይም የምትጠበቅ አይደለችም፡፡

ሰለ ሆነም

ሰለ ሆነም የሰው አገር ትምህርት ከሰው አገር ተሰዶ መጥቶ አንደ አደነቆረን እንደ አደኽየን ሳይሆን የጥንት ኪነ ጥበብን ያስገኙ ኢትዮጵያዊያን ጠቢባን አንደ አመልከቱን ወጣቱን ልናመለክተውና ሊማር ይገባል:: የዓለም ህዝብ የዚሁ ሥርዓት ትምህርት አምቢ አሻፈረኝ ብሎ ስለ ተነሣ ነው አሁን ትንሽ በዕድገት ለመንቀሳቀስ የሞከረው። ሃገር ማለት የሕይወት ለውጥ ነውና። የሱ ተከታዮቸም ተጠቅመዋል ለማለት አይደለም፡፡ ሥርዓቱ ሃገርን ከገደለ በሁዋላ በቅድሚያ የወገኖቹን ዓለም ነው ያወደመባቸዉ፡፡ ምክንያቱም ሥራን የሚያስፈታና የሚያነሆልል ሥውር በሺታ ነውና፡፡

እምነትና ሥርዓት

እነዚህ የቀድሞ ጠቢባን በነበሩበት ጊዜም እምነት ነበረ። ስለዚህ እምነት አያስፈልግም ማለት አይደለም። እምነት ክመጀመርያዉ ሰው ጋር የተገኘ ነውና። እምነትና ሥርዓተ ምንጫቸዉ የተለያየ ነው። እምነት፡ በዘር ጊዜ የተገኘ ነው፡ የሱ ሥርዓት፡ በመኽር ጊዜ የተገኘ ነውና። በኅብረተ ሰብ መሃከል አፈንጋጭነት በነፃነት ፍለጋ የሚጎላ መሣርያ ነው። እውነትና ነፃነት ከሌለበት ሥፍራ ፍልስፍና ወይም ንቃተ ሕሊና ሊዳብር አይችልም የሚል እምነት ቢኖርም፡ ሕግና ሥርዓት ግን መከበር እንዳለበት ማረጋገጥ ይገባል። ሥርዓተ አልበኝነት ለሁሉም መልካም አይደለምና።

ባሕረ ቀመር

ከአኀዝ ቁጥር የተገኘ ባሕረ ቀመር የጥናት ቁጥር፡ ከዚህ ቅድመ ገጽ በተከታታይ ይቀርባል። ቅኔንና ጠንካራ ባህሎችንም ለማቅረብ ይሞከራል። የቅኔና የፍልስፍና ጽሑፍ ባለሙያዎችና ፍላጎት ያላቸዉ ተባባሪ ይሆኑ ዘንድ በከብር ይታደማሉ። ስለ ሆነም የሚቀርቡት የፍልስፍናና ንቃተ ሕሊና ይዘት ያላቸዉ ጽሑፎች ፖለቲካዊና በጣም ነፃነታዊ ይዘትን በውስጣቸዉ ያሠረፁ ቢሆኑ፡ ርቀቱ የአቅራቢዉ ምሥጢር አንጂ የቅድመ ገጹ ምሥጢር አንዳልሆነ ማረጋገጥ ይገባል። ቅድመ ገጹ አሁንም በመጠባበቅ ላይ ነው፡፡

Qene and Reckoning

This page will continue as a page of Qene and a page of Bahre Kemer. Consciousness is born of thinking, little by little: it springs from thought and is founded upon it. Qene, in turn, is a source of reckoning; consciousness and philosophy are therefore kin to Qene. Yet the consciousness held within Qene has found no one to point it toward what would serve itself and the nation, and so it has not been put to work.

Spiritual Teaching

The plan is to weave these pages around this theme. Consciousness, spiritual and worldly alike, is decisive — and above all one must learn to distinguish the Creator from His creatures. The Ethiopian faithful have found no one to teach them this distinction. The young people recruited to teach the Holy Gospel go to the theological college in Addis Ababa and return not knowing their Creator any more clearly, but laden with what leaves them in still greater disarray, coming home only to entrench customary practice. They cannot tell even who created them — their own Creator — and they soften the minds of the faithful to fill them with idolatry. Their striving is like that of the lion and the leopard straining to bring down their prey. They try to persuade people to worship the Creator and the created together, as equals — not with a calm and holy spirit, but with a clamour that resembles an unclean one.

The Teaching Does Not Reach People

The teaching offered today does not pass into people; it does not heal the wounded heart. For its teachers have not laboured to become vessels of the Holy Spirit — and what it means to be such a vessel, only one who has become one knows. Spiritual teaching is given on every corner, by every group that calls itself a faith. In the Tewahedo faith especially, what is offered is not the tradition of the Church fathers — clothed in patience and long held in honour — but something borrowed from the neighbours. Because the teachers themselves have not been broken and refined in spirit, but have grown coarse, they roar like lions and bellow like bulls until people in every neighbourhood are worn out. Shouting cannot be knowledge, nor explanation, nor persuasion.

The Young and Society

Ride the taxis, walk the markets and public places, and watch society — the young above all. Anyone who sees and hears them shove and insult mothers and fathers, pregnant women and the frail would say that, far from seeming born and raised in Ethiopia, they do not seem to have seen as much of Ethiopia as a tourist has. To those of you who have never been inside this, it may sound like reproach and contempt. It is not. It is said in the belief that it will inform those who do not know the truth, and serve the young themselves as spiritual admonition and the counsel of their own people.

For the Nation’s Growth

This page will host and reflect any writing or spoken counsel, grounded in the philosophy and ideas of consciousness, that can bring growth to the nation and awakening to the young. Its aim is to practise the philosophy of consciousness from the very beginning — from ሀ, from one. To teach philosophy and consciousness, one must first learn; and there has never been an authority or a system that forbids learning and knowing. This is not to say that consciousness and philosophy were absent from our institutions of higher learning, or are absent now — only to practise them here.

The Arts

Still, it is through the arts that this shows most clearly — for every movement in our world is art. Those who woke a little earlier and crowd around the seat of administration call it rebellion when art and philosophy cast the rays of something new. To itself and to the society to come it is a ray and a sign of light; but to those who do not understand, it is rebellion. Among the few great arts able to reflect the mysteries of nature are the philosophies discovered long ago, painting, music, drama, Qene, literature and the like.

The Calling of Artists

The calling of artists reaches beyond consciousness: on every road of growth and civilisation it serves as strength, like iron and cement. Above all, it means moving — spiritually, materially or through science — set apart in conscience from the society of the day, or in revolt against it. The work of art is always hidden; it never stands out to be seen. If the young are not shown such a direction, they will sit where they are, and far from building up the country they will become a burden upon it. A country is protected and grows through the citizens who emerge, refined, from its society; it does not live by hanging from a single president or prime minister, nor is it kept safe by one.

Therefore

We should therefore guide the young — and they should learn — not in the way that foreign education, exiled here from foreign lands, has deafened and impoverished us, but in the way the Ethiopian sages who brought forth the ancient arts showed us. It is because the peoples of the world rose up and refused this system of education — “No, I will not!” — that they have now managed to move a little toward progress. For a country means a change of life. Nor is it to say that the system’s own followers have benefited: after it killed the country, it was first its own adherents’ world that it destroyed — for it is a hidden disease that leaves people idle and stupefied.

Faith and Order

In the days of those ancient sages there was faith as well, so this is not to say that faith is unnecessary; faith came into being with the first human. Faith and its order spring from different sources: faith came at the time of sowing; its order came at the time of harvest. Within a society, dissent is an instrument that comes to the fore in the search for freedom. And though it is held that philosophy and consciousness cannot develop where there is no truth or freedom, it must be affirmed that law and order should be respected — for lawlessness is good for no one.

Bahre Kemer

The Bahre Kemer study numbers, derived from the Ge’ez numerals, will be presented on this page in instalments, and we will also try to present Qene and enduring traditions. Specialists in Qene and in philosophical writing, and all who share the interest, are cordially invited to collaborate. Accordingly, should any writings on philosophy and consciousness presented here carry political or strongly freedom-minded content, it should be understood that how far they go is the contributor’s own concern, not this page’s. The page remains open and waiting.
