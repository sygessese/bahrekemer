አዲስ አምላክ ቢኖርIf There Were a New God

ይትባረክ ገሠሠ መራBy Yitbarek Gessese Merra

ባሕረ ቀመር ኪነ ጥበባት ማዕከል — ከይትባረክ ገሠሠ መራ፡ የቅኔ መምህር፡ ሠዓሊና ደራሲ የ አኀዝ ቊጥር ተመራማሪBahre Kemer, a Centre for the Arts — by Yitbarek Gessese Merra, teacher of Qene, painter, author and researcher of Ge’ez numerals

መንግሥቱና ሌኒንMengistu and Lenin

መንግሥቱና ሌኒን ካህናት የተባሉ በቁመት በዕውቀት ይመሳሰላሉ የዘውድ ታሪክን መገልበጥ ያውቃሉ የፈጠራ ድርሰት የላቸው ዕድሉ ግልብጡን ትተውት ርቀው ኮበለሉ የሀገር ሺፍታ እንጂ አልነበሩም መሪ ኢዲአሚን ዳዳ መንግሥቱ ኑሜሪ መሪ እንዴት ይሸሻል የነበረ አስፈሪ ቀሚስ ነበር እንጂ አልታጠቁም ሱሪ ሱሪ የአፍሪካ ወንድነት አብሣሪ ሀገር የሚጠብቅ የሥልጣን መሰሪ
Mengistu and Lenin, who were called priests, are alike in stature and in learning; they know how to overturn the history of the crown, but the gift of creative writing is not theirs. They left what they had overturned and fled far away. They were bandits of the country, not leaders — Idi Amin Dada, Mengistu, Numeiri. How can a leader flee, who once was feared? It was a skirt they wore; they never girded on trousers — trousers, the herald of African manhood, that guard the country, the craft of power.

የባቲካን ንጉሥThe King of the Vatican

የቀድሞዉን ሳይሆን የአሁኑ አዲስ የሮማ ከተማ የባቲካን ንጉሥ የዓለሙ ጥላ ዮሐንስ ጰውሎስ ዓለማችን አጣች የነበራት ሞገስ እሱ ብቻ ነበር እንባን የሚያብስ ደፍሮ በመንግሥቱ ዓመፅ የሚያኮስስ እሱ አልነበረም ወላዋይ እንደቄስ ዝም ብሎ የሚያይ መብት ሲደመስስ እንደ የኛ ቄሶች ክህነታቸው ሲፈርስ በሶሻሊስት ዜማ ክህነት ቴዎፍሎስ የኛ ድልድይ ሸክላ ሸክላ የተሠራ በሱ ተሻግረን ስንበላ እንጀራ ገና በዚያው ሳለን በሱ ስናወራ ድልድዩ ተሰብሮ ኢንጅነር ተጠራ ተሰብሮ ወደቀ ሸክላ ስለነበር ያማረ የነበር እኛ ስንሻገር ከዚያማ ጉድፍ ነው ጣሉት ከመቃብር፡ እኛ ስንመለስ በኄድንበት እግር ወደ ነበርንበት ወደ እኛ ሀገር እንጀራ ስንበላ የውርደት የችግር ድልድዩ ቢሠራም ፈጥሮአል ጥርጥር መልሶ በድንገት ደርሶ እንዳይሠበር
Not the former one, but the present, the new one: the king of the Vatican, in the city of Rome, the shade of the world, John Paul. Our world has lost the grace it had; he alone was the one who wiped away tears, who dared to shame the violence of Mengistu. He was not wavering, like a priest who looks on in silence while rights are crushed, as when the priesthood of our own priests fell apart — the priesthood of Theophilos, to the socialist tune. Our bridge, built of clay upon clay — we crossed over by it, eating injera; while we were still there, talking of it, the bridge broke, and an engineer was called. It broke and fell, because it was clay. It was beautiful while we were crossing; after that it is rubbish — they threw it into the grave. When we return on the feet we went on, to where we were, to our own country, eating the injera of disgrace and of hardship, though the bridge is rebuilt, it has raised a doubt that it may suddenly break again.

የልጅ ባልThe Child’s Husband

ኢትዮጵያ ነበሯት አሥራ አራት ልጆች፡ አንዷን ዳረችና ጠላትን አፈራች ሁሉም ኮበለሉ የተቀሩት ወንዶች የታመሙም አሉ እሷ አልታደለች ተሰብስበው መጥተው ሊያክሟት ዶክተሮች፡ የሚቆራርጡ እግርንና እጆች
Ethiopia had fourteen children; she gave one in marriage, and bred an enemy. The sons who remained all fled; some fell sick — she was not blessed. Doctors gathered and came to treat her, doctors who cut off legs and hands.

የሰላም አዝመራThe Harvest of Peace

ኧረ ሃይማኖቶች ምንድር ነው ሚያወሩ፡ በየደረሱበት አምላክ የሚፈጥሩ፡ ብርሃናት ቢሆኑም ከሁሉም የሚያምሩ፡ የሱ ሥራ ናቸው ሳትጠራጠሩ፡ እንኳን የአምላክ ቤት ሰዎች ተባበሩ፡ በኒዮርክ ከተማ ትልቅ መቅደስ ሰሩ፡ ሁሉ ሚሰግዱበት ሳለ በሃገሩ፡ አጭርና ረዥም ሳይፎካከሩ፡ ቀጭንና ወፍራም አብረው ሊዘምሩ፡ ጥቁርና ቢጫ ነጭም እንደ ኅብሩ፡ አንስማማም ብለው ረጃጅም ሳይኮሩ፡ አብረው ሊኮፈሱ አጫጭር ሳያፍሩ፡ አብረው ይኖራረሉ ፍቅርን እያፈሩ፡፡
Oh, religions, what is it you are saying, creating a god wherever you arrive? The lights, though they are lovelier than all things, are His work — do not doubt it. Even the people of God’s house have united; in the city of New York they built a great sanctuary, where all may bow down, though each is in his own country: short and tall, without rivalry, thin and stout, to sing together, black and yellow and white, like its colours; the tall not growing proud, saying, “We will not agree,” the short not ashamed to stand proud beside them — they live together, bearing the fruit of love.

የአምላክ ጐረቤትGod’s Neighbour

የሰማይ አዳራሽ የሌለው መሠረት፡ የሰው ልጅ ዘንድሮ ያለው ከዚህ መሬት፡ ሰብዓዊ ተፈጥሮው ሰይጣን ዓደንቁሮት፡ መሠረት የሌለው ያዘጋጀውን ቤት፡ እያሽሞነመነ ድንገተኛው ምፅዓት፡ በሳይንስ ተሠርቶ በሳይንስም ትምህርት፡ መንደር ሊሠራ ነው ለመኖር በርቀት፡ በቅርብ ለመሆን የአምላክ ጎረቤት፡ አቤት የቆየ ሰው ውጤቱን ለማየት፡ የሃብታም ፈረሶች ክንፍን የተዋቡት፡ የበረሩ እንደሆን በዚያ በከተሙት፡ አቤት ይሠውረን ከደፋሮች ጥፋት፡፡ ማንኛውም ነገር በዚህ ዓለም ሲገኝ፡ ተቃራኒ አለው እሱን የሚመቀኝ፡ ጨለማና ብርሃን ለምን አይገናኝ፡፡ ሰባት አዳራሾች ለእያንዳንዱ ብቻ፡ አንድ ሺ ይዛሉ ያለአንዳች ፍራቻ፡ ስምነተኛይቱ ግን አልሆነችም አቻ፡ ሃያ ይጎድላታል ዝቅ ብላ ለብቻ፡፡ ከዚያ በኋላማ ይሆናል እምርታ፡ ከአሮጌው መሬት ወደ አዲሱ ቦታ አቤት ይሠውረን ከደፋሮች ጌታ፡፡ ሳይመዝ ሐረጉን ከሰለሞን ዘውድ፡ ባልጎ ያደገ ቀድሞ ሲወለድ፡ ቁልቁል ገለበጠው የንጉሥን ማዕድ ያውቅ ስለነበር እንደሚሰደድ፡ ገልባጭ ተገልብጦ በፈረደው ፍርድ፡ አጥማጁን ትይዛለች ግፈኛን ወጥመድ፡፡ እንጀራን ሳይበሉ ጠላንም የሚያምር፡ ከዚህ ተለይቶ ርቆ የሚኖር፡ ሕይወት አይባልም ሕይወት የፍጡር፡ ዶሮ ወጥ እንጀራ ጠጀም ያ ክቡር፡ ባንድ ላይ በመሶብ ሲበላ በፍቅር፡ እንዴት ያስደስታል የውበት ማማር፡ በሣሃን ጨልፎ መብላት እንዳሽከር፡ ክቡራን ተብለው ጻፊና ዶክተር፡፡ በፈረስ በመንፈስ የመጣች ተጉዛ፡ ለዓለም ሕጻናት ልትሆናችው ቤዛ፡፡ ዲሞክራሲ እናት ሕያው እንደ ዋዛ፡፡
The hall of heaven, which has no foundation — mankind, which this year is upon this earth, its human nature deafened by Satan, the house it has prepared, which has no foundation, decking it out — the sudden Coming — built by science and by the teaching of science, it is about to build a village, to live far away, to be close by, a neighbour of God. Oh, to live long enough to see what comes of it! The horses of the rich, adorned with wings — if they fly to where they have settled, oh, may He hide us from the ruin of the reckless. Everything, when it is found in this world, has an opposite that envies it — why do darkness and light not meet? Seven halls, each one on its own, hold a thousand, without any fear; but the eighth was not their equal: she lacks twenty, set lower, apart. After that there will be a leap, from the old earth to the new place — oh Lord, hide us from the reckless. Without drawing his lineage from Solomon’s crown, one who grew up humbly from his birth turned the king’s table upside down, for he knew that he would be driven out. The overturner was overturned by the judgment he passed; the snare of the cruel catches its own setter. Without eating injera, and the fine tella, living apart from here, far away, is not called life — the life of a creature. Doro wot and injera, and that honoured tej, eaten together from the mesob, in love — how delightful, how beautiful! Scooping from a plate to eat, like a servant, are those called honourable, the writer and the doctor. She came, journeying on horseback and in spirit, to be a ransom for the children of the world: Democracy, the mother, alive, as if it were nothing.

ወዳጅ ሲክድWhen a Friend Betrays

ፍቅረኛ ምላስህ ረጅም የነበረ፡ ምነው ተጣመመ አሁንስ አጠረ፡ ልቤ ነው ፊቴ አሁን የጠቆረ፡ ውስጣዊ አካህ ይሸሸኝ ጀመረ፡፡ ይኽ ጥቁር ካባ ከእንጨት ተስቅሎ፡ ማጅራት መቺ ነው የሚለምን መስሎ፡፡ አሁን አብረው ሳሉ ብርሃንን ሲያፈልቅ፡ ጨለማ ፈጣሪ ያሉት በስላቅ፡ አገርን ያጠፋ ወገን በመናቅ፡ ኋላ ተለይቶ ወደ ሞት ሲርቅ፡ ኪነ ጥበባትን ተሸክሞ ወርቅ፡ ተከትሎ መሮጥ የሱን ሥራ ብርቅ፡ አሁን ምን ያደርጋል አሁን መጨነቅ፡ በሕይወት ሳሉ ነው መርገም መመረቅ፡፡ ጠንቀቅ ጠንቀቅ በሉ ደብተራና ቄስ፡ እንደ ገብረ ሐና ኋላ እንዳትረክስ፡ ዲያቆንም ንቃ ንቃ አትልከስከስ፡ ኋላ በተራራ ሲሠሩ መቅደስ፡ አመዳም ይሉሃል እንዳትመለስ፡፡ ገበሬ ነጋዴ ታቦት አይባሉ፡ ደብተራና ቄስም መምህራኑ ሁሉ፡ በሴት ምክንያት ነው ኃጥአን በሙሉ፡ እነዚያ ብቻ ነው ጽድቁን የሚበሉ፡ ከነገሥታት ጥቂት ማኅበርተኛም አሉ፡ ቀድሞ በማዕድ አብረው ስለ በሉ፡፡
Lover, your tongue that was once so long — why has it twisted, why has it grown short now? It is my heart, my face, that has darkened now; your inner self has begun to shun me. This black cloak hung upon a stick is a mugger, though it seems to be begging. Now, while they were together, while he brought forth light, they called him a creator of darkness, in mockery, one who ruined the country, despising his own people. Later, when he parted, going far away into death, carrying the gold of the arts, to run after his rare work — what good does it do now, this worrying now? It is while one lives that one is cursed or blessed. Take care, take care, debtera and priest, lest you be defiled later, like Gebre Hanna. And you, deacon, wake up, wake up, do not go fawning; later, when they build a sanctuary on the mountain, they will call you ash-grey, so that you may not come back. Farmers and merchants are not called the Tabot, nor debtera and priest, nor all the teachers; because of woman, they are sinners, all of them; only those are the ones who eat of righteousness; among kings there are a few fellow members too, for they ate at table together before.

ብርዕየ ለባዕድ አይምበረከክምMy Pen Does Not Kneel to a Stranger

ጠንቃቃው ብርዕየ መጽሐፍን አይቶአል ስግደትና እምነት ለማን ይገባዋል፡ ኢሳያስ ነቢይ የታመነው በቃል፡ ምዕራፍ ዐርባ ሁለት ቊጥር ስምንት ይላል፡ ክብርየን አልሰጥም ለፍጥሩ አካል፡ ታዲያ ምነው አባ ኮርቶ ሚሰተካከል፡ እኩል ሊመሰገን እንደ አማኑኤል፡ ሰቀላንም ሠርቶ ከዚያ የሚቆለል፡፡ ምናልባት ግብዞች ድንገት ቢታለሉ፡ እኔ አልጨነቅ በሚሆነው ሁሉ፡ ሰማንያ ላሞ አሉኝ የተስተካካሉ፡ የቀሩትን ላሞች ቢውጣቸው ቅሉ፡ በዮሴፍ ቃላት ተጠብቀው ያሉ፡ የፍጥረቱ ነገሥት ሥላሴ ያውቃሉ፡ ምስክረነቴ ለሱ ነው ለግሉ፡፡
My careful pen has looked into the Book: to whom are worship and faith due? Isaiah the prophet, faithful in his word, says in chapter forty-two, verse eight: “My glory I will not give to a created being.” Why then does the Abba, grown proud, make himself equal, to be praised alike, as Emmanuel is, building a tall house and perching himself up there? Perhaps, should the hypocrites suddenly be deceived, I do not worry about whatever comes to be: I have eighty cows, well matched; even if the remaining cows were swallowed, they are kept safe by the words of Joseph. The Trinity, King of creation, knows; my testimony is for Him alone.

የአእምሮ ወተትThe Milk of the Mind

የኢትዮጵያ ጥጆች ወተትን ይጥገቡ፡ መምህራን አልሕምትም ተግተው ይታለቡ፡ የተጣራም ወተት ጥጆች ይመገቡ፡ ወተት ካልጠገቡ ለእርሻ አይረቡ፡፡ በየደጀ ሰላም ያለው ትልቅ ክምር ትንሽ በትንሹ ካልቀነሱት በፍቅር ሰፊ አውድማ ተበጅቶ ባገር በተለይ ዘንድሮ የከተማ አድባ፡ በቅርብ ዓመታት ሳይሞላ ፻ ወር ሳይወቃ አይቀርም ባውድማ መንደር፡፡
Let Ethiopia’s calves drink their fill of milk; let the teachers, the cows, be milked with diligence; let the calves feed on milk that is refined; calves that have not had their fill of milk are no use for the plough. The great heap that lies at every church gate, unless they lessen it, little by little, with love — a wide threshing floor having been made in the land, especially this year, the city’s guardian, within the coming years, before ፻ months are full, it will surely be threshed in the threshing-floor village.

ኪነ ጥበባትThe Arts

Every work of the arts has a great power of mind, able to carry it from where it is all the way to the universe. For the growth of a society it is like cement and concrete: it holds the foundation of the country and all its movement.

Though it is known to be the measure and the proof of their growth, all those who rose after the order of Emperor Haile Selassie, being gofere, gave it no value at all.

የሕሊና ገመድThe Cord of the Mind

የሙዚቃ ገመድ ተገምዶ በጆሮ፡ ሕሊናን ለማሠር አዙሮ አዙሮ፡ ውስጣዊ መነፅር ሕሊናን መርምሮ፡ ሰዎችን በሞላ በረቂቅ አሥሮ፡ የሚያገናኛቸውን እውነት ተናግሮ፡ ዋሽቶ ቢሆን እንኴን ያድሰዋል መክሮ፡ ዘመድም ወዳጅም ሲደሰት አብሮ፡ ጊዜን ያሳጥራል በጆሮ አስክሮ፡፡ የሠዓሊ ጣቶች የዓይን ዘመድ ምሥጢርና ጥበብ የሚገማምድ የሰውን ሕሊና የሚያስተናግድ፡ ብሩሽና ቀለም አብሮ በመጥመድ አዝመራ የዓይን ደርሶ እንዲወድ በጥበብ ላይ ጥበብ ተጨንቆ መውለድ የምሥጢርን ጅራፍ ሠርቶ የሚያነጒድ፡ የሰውን ሕሊና ስቦ ከምንገድ፡ ጐትቶ ጐትቶ የሚያስተናግድ፡፡ ይሄ መንገደኛ የፀሐፊ ብዕር፡ አቤት ያለው ብርታት ወሬን ለመከመር፡ ኃዘን ይሁን ደስታ ጧት ለመናገር ሰዉ ሳይነሳ የሚናገር ሚሥጢር፡ ብዕር የማያውቃት ያላያትን መንደር፡ በዓለሙ ሁሉ ፈጽማ አትኖር፡ ከማንኛውም ቤት ከማኛው ሀገር፡ በጆሮ በመስኮት ሳያስፈልገው በር፡ ዘሎ እየገባ ያላንዳች ክርክር፡ ቃላትን መንዝሮ ወሬን ሲቸረችር፡ በጣም ያስገርማል ያሲዛልም ፍቅር፡፡
The cord of music, twisted into the ear, winding round and round to bind the mind; an inner lens, searching the mind, binding all people with something unseen, speaking the truth that joins them together; even had it lied, it would renew him with its counsel; when kin and friend rejoice together, it shortens the time, making the ear drunk. The painter’s fingers, kin to the eye, that twist mystery and wisdom into one cord, that give the human mind a welcome; yoking brush and paint together, so the harvest of the eye may ripen and be loved; labouring to give birth to art upon art, making a whip of mystery that cracks, drawing the human mind in from the road, pulling and pulling it in, to welcome it. This traveller, the writer’s pen — oh, what strength it has to heap up the news! Be it sorrow or joy, to tell it in the morning, it tells the secret before people have risen. A village that the pen does not know, has not seen, does not exist at all in the whole world. Into any house, into any country, by ear, by window, needing no door, it leaps in without any argument, changing words like coins, retailing the news — it is a great wonder, and it makes one fall in love.

የዓለም ፍርድThe World’s Judgment

ሂትለር ለኩነኔ ማንዴላ ለጽድቅ፡ ዓለሙ ተስማማ ምሥጢሩን የሚያውቅ፡ ሉተርን ከምዕራብ ጋንዲን ከምሥራቅ፡ ትሬዛ ከመሃል ፈጥኖ መጠየቅ፡ በኅብረት ሰላምን አይቶ ለማረቅ፡ ቱቱሳ ምንድር ነው የሚዘባረቅ፡ ፖለቲካን ደርሶ በገፍ የሚዝቅ፡ ዓለም በቃኝ ብሎ መሾሙን ሳያውቅ፡ ተፈሪን ንገሩት እንዳይጨነቅ፡ ሥራውን ሳይጨርስ እኛን ማስታረቅ፡ ፈጥኖ ስለኄደ ሳያዘጋጅ ስንቅ፡ ለዱርየ ትቶ ኢትዮጵያችን ወርቅ ዘውድና መስቀልን ሳይጠነቀቅ፡ ጳጳሱ በጉድጎድ አስከሚደበቅ፡፡ ከሰዎች መሀከል ብቻዬን ስናገር፡ ሰዎቹ አይሰሙኝ እንደ ልዩ ፍጡር፡ ሰዉ የሞላ ነው እንደ ዛፍና ሣር፡ ከዚያ መሃል ሁሉ ብቻዬን ስማረር፡ ብቻዬን ያለሁ ይመስለኛል ከዱር፡ ምክንያቱም ሰዉ ሰውን አያፈቅር፡ ገፍቶ ገፋፍቶ የሚጥል ከችግር፡፡
Hitler for damnation, Mandela for righteousness: the world agreed, the world that knows the secret. Luther from the West, Gandhi from the East, Teresa from the middle — quick to ask, seeing peace in union, to set things right. And Tutu — what is it he rambles about, reaching into politics and scooping it up in heaps, not knowing of his appointment, having renounced the world? Tell Teferi not to be troubled that he did not finish his work of reconciling us; because he went quickly, without preparing provisions, leaving our Ethiopia, gold, to the hoodlum, without taking care of crown and cross, until the patriarch hid in a pit. When I speak alone among people, the people do not hear me, as if I were a strange creature; it is full of people, like trees and grass; in the midst of it all, when I complain alone, I feel as though I am alone in the wilderness; for man does not love man — he pushes and shoves and throws him down into hardship.

የማያልቅ ፍሬThe Fruit That Never Runs Out

ከምሥራቅ አፍሪካ የበቀለች ዛፍ፡ ፍሬዋ በብዛት የሚንዘፈዝፍ፡ ኧረ አሁንስ በዛ እሷን የሚቀጥፍ፡ በግም ይሁን ፍየል የቆመው በሰልፍ፡ እሷን ቀጥፎ ቀጥፎ ፍሬን ሲያራግፍ፡ ሁሉ ተሽኮርማሚ ሁሉም የሚያኮርፍ፡ አሁን ይባስ ብሎ የወታደር ጎርፍ፡ አርቆ ለመኄድ በእምነት ክንፍ፡ ወይም በሳይንስ የማይሰለፍ፡ ፍሬዋ እንደሆን ለእልፍ አእላፍ፡ ይበቃል አያልቅም ካልሆነ ሰነፍ፡
A tree that sprang up in East Africa, its fruit hanging heavy in abundance — oh, now there are too many plucking it! Sheep or goat, standing in line, plucking and plucking it, shaking down the fruit; all of them coy, all of them sulking; and now, worse still, a flood of soldiers — to go far on the wings of faith, or not to line up with science — her fruit, if it be for elf a’elaf, thousands upon thousands, is enough and never runs out, unless one is lazy.

ጠማማ ምላስA Twisted Tongue

ውለታ ክፉ ነው ሰውን ያታልላል፡ ከበላ ከጠጣ ምላስ ይጣመማል፡ በጣም ክፎች ናቸው መጠጥና መብል፡ ሰውስ ይቅርና ለማየቱ ቀላል፡ የማይታየውን አምላክን ያሳያል፡
A favour is a bad thing; it deceives a man. Once he has eaten and drunk, the tongue twists. Very bad things they are, drink and food: never mind a man, who is easy to see — they show you God, who cannot be seen.

ብሥራትGlad Tidings

ርገበ ኖኅ ፓስታ በዓይኁ መከራ፡ ቄጠማ ሰላምታን በአፍዋ ቀርቅራ፡ ሰላም ለሁላችሁ ሰለማችሁ ይብራ፡ እያለች በደስታ ከተፍ አለች በራ፡ እንደ ባለ ክንፉ የገነት አሞራ፡ ወደ ትዝታዬ ያደግሁባት ስፍራ፡፡ አልዘነጋም እኔ የናቴን ውለታ፡ እኔን ለማሳደግ መከራዋን አይታ፡ ውሃን ተሸክማ እንጨትንም ለቅማ፡ በክረምት ወራት እኔም በደረቷ፡ ምንም ሳይለየኝ ፍቅረኛዬ ጡቷ፡፡
Noah’s dove, the post, in the eye of hardship, holding fresh grass, a greeting, in her beak, “Peace to you all, may your peace shine,” she said, and alighted with joy, suddenly, in flight, like the winged bird of paradise, into my memory, the place where I grew up. I do not forget my mother’s kindness: she bore hardship to raise me, carrying water and gathering wood, and I, in the months of the rains, at her breast — nothing parted me from her breast, my beloved.

አንድ ሳሉ የተጣሉQuarrelling Though One

ጁዊሽና ቡድሀ ካቶሊክ ኦርቶዶክሰ፤ እስላም ፕሮቴስታንት ኢባንጂሊካንስ፤ ሌላም ሌላም ሁሉ በባሕርና በየብስ፤ የሚገዛው ማን ነው በሥጋና በነፍስ፤ እሱ አይደለም ወይ ጥበበኛው ንጉሥ፤ ሲፈልግ የሚክብ ሲፈልግ የሚያፈርስ፤ ብርሃንን ለሁሉ እኩል የሚደግስ፤ እነቶኔን ሁሉ የሚፈረካክስ፤ አሁን የሚበጀው ፍቅር ንጹሕ መንፈስ፤ በኅብረት መጸለይ በኅብረት መደነስ፤ ከዚህ ሁሉ ክፉ የሰው ደምን ማፍሰስ፤ ደም እያፈሰሱ የራስን መኮፈስ፤ የእውነት አይደለም ካልሆነ ለመርከሰ፤ እናንተ መሪዎች ዲያቆንና ቄስ፤ ምስክር ፍጠሩ የማያድበሰብስ፤ እውነት የሚናገር ልብን የሚፈውስ፤፤ በዓለም ያለችሁ ቸርች የምትሄዱ፤ በርታ በርታ በሉ ቀና ነው መንገዱ፤ በጰውሎስ ትምሀርት ለምትገደዱ፤ ተንጓሎ ይውጣ ፍሬውና ግርዱ፡፡
Jewish and Buddhist, Catholic, Orthodox, Muslim, Protestant, Evangelicals, and all the others, on sea and on land — who is it that rules them, in body and in soul? Is it not He, the wise King, who builds when He wills and tears down when He wills, who spreads a feast of light for all alike, who shatters every so-and-so? What serves now is love, a pure spirit: to pray together, to dance together. Worst of all this is shedding human blood — shedding blood and puffing oneself up; it is not of the truth, unless it be to defile. You leaders, deacon and priest, bring forth a witness who will not blur things, who speaks the truth and heals the heart. You throughout the world who go to church, say, “Take heart, take heart” — the road is straight; you who are bound by the teaching of Paul, let the grain and the chaff be sifted apart.

የናዝሬት መሐንዲስThe Engineer of Nazareth

አቤት ያለው ጥበብ በእጁ በቃሉ፤ የናዝሬት መሐንዲስ የዓለሙም ሁሉ፤ ዓሥራ ሁለት መብራት በሱ ተተከሉ፤ ቀስ በቀስ አምቱሎች በርተው ሲቃጠሉ፤ ፈጽሞ አይጨልም ይታያል ፀዳሉ፤ እሱ ስለሆነ የመብራት ኃይሉ፡፡ ጴጥሮስም በአቅሙ ክዋክብትን ዘራ፤ ከጋኑ ከምንጩ ጎትቶ ሳይፈራ፤ የብርሃን ወታደር በእውነት ጠንካራ፤ ለሁሉ ሚበቃ አዳራሽ አሠራ፤ ጣሪያና መሠረት በውል የተጣራ፤፤ አረጀ የሚሉ የማያውቁ ጽድቅ፤ መንፈስ ቅዱስ አለ መጠገን የሚያውቅ፤ የሁሉ መሐንዲስ ሳይንቲስት ረቂቅ፤ ጧት እስከ ማታ ከዚያች የማይለቅ፤ ሲፈለግ ብርሃኑን የሚፈነጥቅ፤ ቀድሞም የነበረ ናዝሬት በድብቅ፤ መንፈስና ደምን በመደበላለቅ፤ ንጉሥና አባት ሆኖ እስከሚልቅ፤ ጴጥሮስ ያልቻላትን ያቺን ጠባብ ሠርቅ፤ እሱ ግን ፈተናት በመቃብር ንድቅ፤ ለምስክርነት አንድ ቃል እንዳይወድቅ፤ መላእክትም መጡ ተዘምዝመው ወርቅ፤ በደመና ፈረስ ልጓም የመብረቅ፤ ዕርገትን ጋልቦ ለማጠናቀቅ፤፤ የወንጌልን ቃላት ፍሬ የመንፈስ፤ የቀመሰ ሁሉ ሳያድበሰበሰ፤ ለምንም ለምንም ይሆነዋል ፈውስ፤ የሚጋልብ ሁሉ በስሕተት ፈረስ፤ ለጓም ስሌለው ከምንም አይደርስ፤ አሁን ተመለሱ በዚህ ንገሥግሥ፤ የወንጌልን ፍሬ እንቀማምስ፤ ሥርዓት ስለ ሆነ ለማየት ንጉሥ፤ ፍሬውን ሳይቀምስ እውነቱን ሳይለበስ፤ መግባት አይቻልም ከንጉሥ ድግስ፡፡
Oh, what wisdom He has, in His hand and in His word — the engineer of Nazareth, and of all the world! Twelve lamps were set up by Him; little by little, as the bulbs light up and burn, it never grows dark; their radiance is seen, because He is the lamps’ power. Peter too, by his strength, sowed stars, drawing from the vessel, from the spring, without fear; a soldier of light, strong in truth, he had a hall built, enough for all, its roof and its foundation made sound. Those who say “it has grown old” do not know righteousness; there is the Holy Spirit, who knows how to mend; engineer of all, the subtle scientist, who never leaves it from morning till night, who scatters His light when it is needed; He who was of old, in Nazareth, in secret, mingling spirit and blood, until He grew to be King and Father; that narrow passage which Peter could not open, He opened it, in the built tomb, so that not one word of the testimony should fall; angels came too, girded with gold, on a horse of cloud, with a bridle of lightning, riding to complete the Ascension. The words of the Gospel, the fruit of the Spirit — everyone who has tasted them, without blurring them, finds in them a cure for everything; everyone who rides a horse in error, since it has no bridle, reaches nowhere. Now come back — let us hurry along this way, let us taste together the fruit of the Gospel; since it is the rule, to see the King, without tasting the fruit, without putting on the truth, one cannot enter the King’s feast.

የአንድ ኅብረተሰብ የዕድገት የቴክኖሎጂ አፍ መክፍቻው የመጀመሪያው ሥነ ጹሑፍና ሙዜቃ ናቸው፡፡ አንድ ሕፃን አፉን ሲከፍት ያልተሟላ ቃላትን ይጠራል እንደዚሁም ኅብረተሰብ ወደ ዕድገት ማምራት ሲጀምር መዝፈን ያልተሰተከከለ ሥነ ጽሑፍ ይቦርቃል ከየት ወዴት የታሪክ ምንጭ የመሠረተ ቋንቋ ዓረፍተ ነገር ይሄን ሁሉ አይመረምርም፡፡ ኋላ እየሰከነ ሲመጣ ከኋላው ታሪኩን እየመዘዘ ሥነ ጹሑፍም መሠረት እየያዝ ይመጣል፡፡ ሙዚቃም እንደ ጥንቸል ሲዘል የነበረ የሙዚቃ ርካታው ሲከናወንለት እንደ በግ አጎንብሶ መኄድና ምሥጢርን መመርመር ይጀምራል፡፡

The first things to open the mouth of a society’s growth and technology are literature and music. As a child, when it first opens its mouth, utters incomplete words, so a society, when it begins to move toward growth, sings, and an unpolished literature bursts out; it does not examine where it comes from and where it is going, the source of its history, the sentence of the foundations of its language — none of this. Later, as it settles, drawing out its history behind it, literature too comes to take hold of a foundation. And music, which had been leaping like a hare, once its musical fulfilment is accomplished, begins to walk bowed like a sheep and to search into mystery.

ከዚህ በኋላ ሥዕልና ኅብረ ጥበብ በየእንቅስቃሴው ጎራ ጎራ እያለ የቴክኖሎጂ ምሥረታ ያስረዳል፡፡ በአሁኑ ጊዜ ሥዕልና ሠዓሊ በኢትዮጵያ ውስጥ አለ ለማለት አይቻልም፡፡ ምክንያቱም ሥዕል የቴክኖሎጂ ጥላ ነው፡፡ በሌላ ወገን ቴክኖሎጂ የሥዕልና የኅብረ ጥበብ ፍሬ ነውና፡፡ ሥዕልና ሠዓሊ አለ ለማት የምንችለው ሠዓሊዉ በሥዕሉ ክብር ከዝቅተኛው ኅብረተ ሰብ ልቆ ሲታይ ነው፡፡ የሠዓሊው ኑሮ የአንድ ሀገር የሳይንስ የቴክኖሎጂና በጠቅላ የዕድገት መለኪያ ቁና ናቸው፡፡ ምክንያቱም ሳይንስ የሚመሠረተው በኪነ ጥበብ ስሚንቶነትና አርማታነት የሰው አእምሮ ስለሚጠነክር ነው፡፡ ያላደጉ ሃገራት መሪዎች ግን ጎፈሬዎች ስለሆኑ ይንህን ለመረዳት መሠረታዊ ዓቅም የላቸዉም፡፤ ሳያውቁ እኔ አውቃለሁ እንጂ እሱ ያውቀል የሚል ልምድ የላቸዉምና ነው፡፡ ስለ ሆነም ወደ ላይ ፈቀቅ ሳይሉ ካሉበት ደረጃ ሲንከባለላሉ ይኖራሉ፡፡

After this, painting and the combined arts, turning aside in each of their movements, show the founding of technology. At present it cannot be said that there is painting, or a painter, in Ethiopia. For painting is the shadow of technology; and, from the other side, technology is the fruit of painting and the combined arts. We can say that painting and the painter exist only when the painter, by the honour of his painting, is seen to rise above the lowest of society. The painter’s living is the measuring-basket of a country’s science, its technology and, altogether, its growth. For science is founded because the human mind grows strong through the arts, as through cement and concrete. But the leaders of undeveloped countries, being gofere, lack the basic capacity to understand this; for, not knowing, they have no habit of saying anything but “I know” — never “he knows.” And so, without moving a step upward, they go on rolling about where they are.

የሠዓሊ ቡርሽ እርሻ የሚጀምር ሳይንስ ተዘርቶ ቡቃያዉ ሲያምር ሲሰክን ሲረጋ ከመዝናናት መዝሙር በአሁኑ ያለ የመዝምሮች ዜማ በሰልፍ የቆመ ሁሉም ለቁዘማ ወይንም ለመዝለል እንደ ዋልያ እንደ ጋማ ወገብን በመቅጨት እንደ ሠሪ ሸማ የወንዶችን ወኔ የለም የሚያሰማ በፍጹም ጠፍቶአል የወንድነት ግርማ እኔ እንደ መሰለኝ ወንዶቹ አልቀዋል የጐራዴና ጦር የአርበኞቹ ቃል ጠላትን ሳይገድሉ በሩቅ ያሸብራ ቀረርቶን ሽለላን ፈረንጅም ያውቀዋል ሽለላ ከሰማ ድንኳኑን ይነቅላል ቀረርቶን ካቅራራ አበሻ አይቻል በታሪክ በጹሑፍ ብሎ መስክሮአል፡፡
The painter’s brush, which begins the ploughing, science sown, its shoot growing lovely, as it settles and grows still, a song out of rest. The tune of today’s singers: all standing in a row to brood, or to leap like the walia ibex, like a maned steed, twisting the waist like a weaver of shemma; there is none who sounds the courage of men; the majesty of manhood is utterly lost. As it seems to me, the men are finished — the word of the patriots, of sword and spear, that terrified the enemy from afar without killing him. Even the foreigner knows the qererto and the shilela: if he hears the shilela, he pulls up his tent; if the Habesha chants the qererto, he cannot be withstood — so he has borne witness, in history and in writing.
ዝ መዝሙር ኢኮነ ከመ ምእመናን ልሙደ ጽሩይ ውእዉ አኮኑ ፍኖተ ሊቃውንት ነገደ፡፡
Zi mezmur ikone keme mi’imenan limude, tsiruy wi’itu akonu finote liqawint negede.

ይህቺ ጉባኤ ቃና ለባለ ቅኔዎች ቅምሻ ናት፡፡ መተርጎም አላስፈለገም፡፡ ቅኔ የሚያውቅ ትርጉም አይፈልግም፡፡

This Gubae Qana is a taste for those who know Qene. There was no need to translate it: one who knows Qene needs no translation.
